# Changelog

## v2 — 2026-11-08

This version studies inhomogeneous constraints of source-free Lorentzian Einstein–Cartan theory with constant complete Nieh–Yan coupling on fixed compact geometries: regular homogeneous metric counts, nonzero flat-torus modes and their compact integrability conditions, and linear perturbations on selected curved vacuum backgrounds. The changes from v1 are listed below.

### Corrections and withdrawn statements

- Correct the epsilon-form action coefficient to reproduce the scalar Einstein–Hilbert normalization. Use complete NY from one connection, full 24-component torsion elimination, signed GHY and corresponding endpoint representatives.
- Withdraw the former DBR/RBD mode-count difference as evidence of a physical ordering effect. Correct primary-multiplier consistency, isolate cubic contamination in the former local action, and compare both routes on the same quadratic block and on a complete-action scalar control.
- Replace the rank-defect physical scalar-kernel identification by regular full homogeneous metric counts of 5/3/2/2. Distinguish singular static-torus constraints, the volume–shear cone and exact homogeneous families, and identify the Taub rank exceptions.
- Withdraw the former universal four-geometry physical TT spectrum and ghost claims. Separate the sphere TT rough Laplacian, full Nil scalar Laplacian (including its central term), and Sol scalar orbit bound. No universal physical spectrum is reinstated.
- Correct the interpretation of nonstationary path curvature and Sol isometries.

### Retained and added results

- Attribute the homogeneous volume–shear cone to Paper06 and collect the frame, finite-metric and geometric-operator results used from Papers 03EC, 04 and 06 in Appendix E. Retain constant-frame ranks and the specified closed/harmonic one-form results with their domains; add the rotating-frame torus comparison and fixed-quotient finite paths.
- Derive the nonzero flat-torus Fourier action with two positive kinetic polarizations. Connection-retaining and metric descriptions agree in the action and the reduced two-form.
- Derive the second-order compact energy and three translation conditions. Under the stated compact CMC and regularity hypotheses, the Arms–Marsden–Moncrief theorem makes these four conditions necessary and sufficient for local integrability. Give explicit standing-wave and nonparallel two-wave corrections through second order, with all thirteen generated wavevectors.
- Add fixed LRS Nil, Sol and Taub vacuum backgrounds. Construct central-zero and central-nonzero Nil reductions, fiber-zero and infinite dual-orbit Sol reductions, and four low Taub Wigner blocks, with gauges, canonical reductions and reconstruction.
- Derive background-dependent KID counts (Nil 1, Sol 0, Taub 4) and quadratic necessary charges on Nil and Taub. On the fixed Sol BF-01 quotient, apply the Fischer--Marsden--Moncrief no-KID theorem to all real full linearized constraint data in H^s x H^(s-1), integer s >= 6; give the elliptic right inverse and local Cauchy/LC lift in (SL-01)--(SL-05).
- Establish applicability to nonzero-base Gowdy data and real dual-orbit data reconstructed from the common core, including the saved continuous Hermite initial profile; require (SL-07) for infinite orbit sums. Higher-order corrections may contain zero modes and other orbits. Fixed-sector nonlinear families and arbitrary reduced L2 data are not covered.
- Include finite-time examples and an independent four-dimensional Lorenz comparison on the interior of one Sol orbit. Convergence in the reduced fourth-order form, whole-line numerical error bounds, general stability and nonlinear lifting sufficiency on Nil and Taub remain unestablished. The scoped Sol existence theorem does not certify the finite-window numerical trajectories or resolve the reduced operator problem.
